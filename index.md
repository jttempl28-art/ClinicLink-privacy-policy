# ClinicLink Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026

ClinicLink is a mobile patient portal operated by **Integrative Health Carolinas PLLC** (“IHC,” “ClinicLink,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

ClinicLink enables patients and authorized users to access healthcare information and services made available by IHC and other participating healthcare practices, clinics, and healthcare providers (“Participating Providers”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, accessed, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the ClinicLink mobile application and related services.

Because ClinicLink may connect you with healthcare providers other than IHC, your Participating Provider may maintain its own privacy policies and Notice of Privacy Practices. Those policies may also govern the medical information maintained by that provider.

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

The information available through ClinicLink depends on the Participating Provider with which your account is connected and the features that provider makes available.

### Account and Identity Information

We may collect or process information used to establish, authenticate, and maintain your account, including:

- Name
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Account identifiers
- Authentication information
- Patient identifiers
- Healthcare-provider identifiers
- Information used to associate your ClinicLink account with one or more patient profiles

### Health and Medical Information

ClinicLink may process or display health information maintained by Participating Providers, including:

- Appointment information
- Laboratory results
- Medical records
- Clinical encounters and visit information
- Vital signs and health measurements
- Secure messages
- Notifications and reminders
- Treatment-related information
- Other health information made available by your Participating Provider

Some of this information may constitute **Protected Health Information (“PHI”)** under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”).

Where IHC processes PHI on behalf of a Participating Provider, that information will be handled in accordance with applicable law and any applicable agreements between IHC and the Participating Provider.

### Information You Provide

Depending on the functionality made available through ClinicLink, you may provide information including:

- Messages to healthcare providers
- Forms and questionnaire responses
- Photos
- Documents
- Health measurements
- Profile information
- Requests or communications
- Information submitted in connection with healthcare services

### Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect or process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and maintain ClinicLink, including:

- Device type
- Operating system and version
- Application version
- Device or application identifiers
- Push-notification tokens
- IP address
- Authentication and security logs
- Application errors and diagnostic information
- Technical information about application performance

We may use this information to maintain security, diagnose technical issues, provide notifications, and improve ClinicLink.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information processed through ClinicLink to:

- Create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts
- Connect users with Participating Providers
- Link authorized users to appropriate patient profiles
- Display health and medical information
- Provide access to appointments, laboratory results, encounters, messages, and other patient-portal features
- Provide appointment reminders and other healthcare-related notifications
- Allow users to communicate with Participating Providers
- Process information submitted by users through ClinicLink
- Maintain the security and integrity of the application
- Detect and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, misuse, or security incidents
- Troubleshoot technical problems
- Maintain and improve application functionality
- Provide support to users and Participating Providers
- Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and healthcare requirements

**ClinicLink does not use health information obtained through Participating Providers for targeted advertising.**

## 3. Participating Healthcare Providers

ClinicLink is a technology platform that may connect users with multiple independent healthcare providers.

A Participating Provider is generally responsible for the medical record it creates and maintains and for determining how that medical information is used in connection with patient care.

Your Participating Provider may maintain information independently of ClinicLink and may have its own:

- Notice of Privacy Practices
- Privacy policies
- Medical-record retention requirements
- Patient-access procedures
- Privacy-request procedures

Questions regarding your diagnosis, treatment, medical record, or a Participating Provider's use or disclosure of your health information should generally be directed to that provider.

Using ClinicLink with one healthcare provider does not automatically provide another healthcare provider with access to your information unless such access is authorized and permitted by applicable law.

## 4. HIPAA and Protected Health Information

IHC takes the privacy and security of health information seriously.

Where HIPAA applies, PHI processed through ClinicLink will be handled in accordance with applicable HIPAA requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, IHC may process PHI as a healthcare provider or as a service provider or business associate acting on behalf of a Participating Provider.

When IHC acts as a business associate, its handling of PHI may also be governed by a Business Associate Agreement with the applicable Participating Provider.

A Participating Provider's Notice of Privacy Practices provides additional information about how that provider may use and disclose PHI and about rights available to patients under HIPAA.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your medical information.

Information may be disclosed in the circumstances described below.

### Participating Providers

ClinicLink may send information to, receive information from, or provide access to information maintained by the Participating Provider associated with your patient profile.

### Service Providers

We may use third-party technology and service providers that help us operate ClinicLink, including providers of:

- Cloud infrastructure and hosting
- Databases and data storage
- User authentication
- Application infrastructure
- Push notifications
- Email or SMS communications where enabled
- Security services
- Error monitoring and diagnostics
- Technical support

These providers may process information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf and are subject to applicable legal, security, and contractual requirements.

Where required by HIPAA, service providers handling PHI are subject to appropriate Business Associate Agreements or other required safeguards.

### Legal and Regulatory Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law or regulation
- Respond to a valid subpoena, court order, or other lawful process
- Respond to lawful governmental requests
- Protect the security or integrity of ClinicLink
- Investigate fraud, misuse, or unauthorized access
- Protect the rights, safety, or property of users, Participating Providers, IHC, or others
- Meet healthcare regulatory or reporting requirements

### Business Transactions

If IHC or ClinicLink is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, information may be transferred as permitted by applicable law.

Any PHI involved in such a transaction remains subject to applicable healthcare privacy requirements.

## 6. Sale of Personal Information and Advertising

ClinicLink does **not sell Protected Health Information**.

ClinicLink does not use PHI obtained through Participating Providers for third-party targeted advertising.

We do not provide medical information to advertising companies for the purpose of creating advertising profiles based on a user's health conditions or treatment.

## 7. Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, ClinicLink may use a device-specific push-notification token to provide notifications such as:

- Appointment reminders
- New-message alerts
- Healthcare-related reminders
- Account notifications
- Other alerts associated with your Participating Provider

The contents of a notification may be visible on your device's lock screen depending on your device settings.

You are responsible for configuring your device's notification and lock-screen settings according to your privacy preferences.

You may disable ClinicLink notifications through your device settings. Disabling notifications does not prevent you from accessing information within ClinicLink.

## 8. Camera, Photos, and Files

Certain ClinicLink features may allow you to:

- Take a photograph
- Select an existing photograph
- Upload a document
- Attach information to a message or healthcare request

ClinicLink may request permission to access your device's camera, photos, or files when required for a feature you choose to use.

Information you choose to upload may become part of information available to your Participating Provider.

You can manage device permissions through your Android or iOS device settings.

## 9. Data Security

IHC uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed through ClinicLink.

Depending on the systems involved, safeguards may include:

- Encryption
- Authentication controls
- Access controls
- Secure network communications
- Logging and monitoring
- Security controls designed to restrict unauthorized access

We continually work to maintain appropriate protections for the information processed through ClinicLink.

However, no electronic information system, mobile device, network, or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and maintaining appropriate security on devices used to access ClinicLink.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide ClinicLink services
- Maintain accounts
- Support Participating Providers
- Maintain security and audit records
- Fulfill contractual obligations
- Meet medical-record retention requirements where applicable
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Medical records displayed through ClinicLink may be maintained by a Participating Provider rather than by ClinicLink itself.

Deleting a ClinicLink account therefore does not necessarily delete the underlying medical record.

Healthcare providers may be legally or professionally required to retain medical records for specified periods.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the information involved, your location, and applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Request access to personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate personal information
- Request deletion of certain personal information
- Obtain information about how personal information is used or disclosed
- Restrict or object to certain processing
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Where HIPAA applies, you may also have rights concerning PHI, including rights described in your healthcare provider's Notice of Privacy Practices.

Some rights may be limited where information must be retained or processed for treatment, medical-record retention, security, legal, or regulatory purposes.

Privacy requests concerning ClinicLink may be submitted to:

**info@integrativehealthcarolinas.com**

## 13. Children and Minors

ClinicLink may provide access to healthcare information concerning children and minors.

Access to a minor's information is governed by applicable law, authorization requirements, and the policies of the applicable Participating Provider.

Parents, guardians, or other legally authorized representatives may be permitted to access a minor's patient profile where legally permitted.

Certain medical information concerning minors may be subject to additional confidentiality protections and may not be available to a parent or guardian in all circumstances.

ClinicLink is not intended to allow a child to independently create or manage an account where parental, guardian, or other authorization is legally required.

## 14. Authorized Representatives and Multiple Patient Profiles

ClinicLink may allow an authorized user to access more than one patient profile, such as profiles belonging to children, dependents, or other individuals for whom the user has appropriate authorization.

Access to another person's information must be authorized by the applicable Participating Provider and permitted by law.

Users must not attempt to access another individual's health information without authorization.

## 15. Third-Party Services and Links

ClinicLink may integrate with or provide links to services operated by third parties.

A third party may maintain its own privacy policies and terms.

This Privacy Policy does not govern information independently collected by a third-party website or service outside ClinicLink.

## 16. Data Storage and Processing

Information processed through ClinicLink may be stored or processed using secure technology providers and infrastructure located in the United States or other locations permitted by applicable law.

Where applicable, we require appropriate safeguards for third parties that process sensitive or protected information on our behalf.
